Where I cover

Strictly mobile, about 20 minutes' drive from Leyton (E10), so the hour is spent treating rather than in traffic.

Outside that, please message me to check availability.

What you need: a clear 2 × 3 metre floor space for the treatment table.

Parking: A visitor parking permit, driveway, or dedicated parking space must be available upon arrival.

Wear something you can move in, such as shorts and a vest. I bring the table and everything else.

Common reasons people book

Osteopathy is not a promise to fix something in one session. These are the problems I am usually asked to assess.

Back and neck pain

Including stiffness that sits in after a desk week, a long drive, or a flare that will not settle.

Training niggles

Running, lifting, and sport. Tight tissue, irritable tendons, and injuries that are limiting sessions.

Recurring pain

When the same spot keeps going. The visit looks for the driver, then treats what is in front of us.

About Jack

Hi, I'm Jack. I'm a registered osteopath and soft tissue specialist based in Leyton, offering premium mobile care across East London. My approach to rehab is rooted in practical, athlete-minded care: I want you to feel better, move better, and stay better.

Whether you're dealing with a stubborn niggle, loss of range of motion, or ongoing pain from training or desk work, I focus on the cause rather than just the symptom. My goal is to help you keep doing what matters most, with less pain and more confidence in your body.
