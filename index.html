About Jack

Hi, I’m Jack. I’m a registered osteopath and soft tissue specialist based in Leyton. I’ve been practicing for the last few years, offering premium mobile care across East London. My aim is simple: get you out of pain, improve how you move, and help you stay active without the stress of clinic waiting rooms or crowded treatment spaces.

My background is actually a bit different from most practitioners. I originally worked as an electrician. Truthfully, troubleshooting a complex electrical grid isn’t all that different from diagnosing a stubborn injury pattern: look for the root cause, fix the issue properly, and make sure it doesn’t keep failing in the same way.

When you book a session, we don’t just chase the localized pain. I conduct a full biomechanical assessment to identify the root cause of your dysfunction. Depending on what’s driving the issue, that may mean manual therapy, mobility work, movement coaching, or a structured rehab plan to keep the benefits lasting.

By bringing the clinic to your living room, we remove the stress of the commute. You get the exact same clinical-grade treatment, but you get to recover on your own sofa.
