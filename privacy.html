Privacy

OsteoJack is Jack Jackson's mobile osteopathy and sports massage practice in East London. This page says what happens to information you send when you enquire or book.

What I collect

If you message me on WhatsApp or Instagram, I see whatever you choose to send: your name, number, area, and anything you write about your injury. If you book through the online calendar (Cliniko), that form collects the details needed for a clinical appointment, including health history. I use that information to confirm a visit, treat you, and keep a clinical record.

Who else sees it

Cliniko processes online bookings. WhatsApp and Instagram process those messages on their own platforms. I do not sell personal information and I do not pass it to marketers.

How long it is kept

Clinical notes are kept for as long as UK osteopathic record-keeping requires. Enquiry messages that do not become an appointment are deleted when they are no longer needed to reply.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask me to delete enquiry messages. Clinical records may have to be kept even if you would rather they were not. Message me on WhatsApp and say what you want done.

This is a short notice for a one-person practice, not a full legal policy. If something here is unclear, ask before you book.
